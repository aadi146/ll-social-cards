Owner: Aadi (review and scheduling, in Zernio). Claude owns the intel layer, the writing and the recurring strategy review. Agreed 31 Oct 2026.

## What this is for
Organic posts that sound like Aadi across a table: plain English, short, one idea each, built from his own recorded views. Organic builds identity and trust; paid (from Dec/Jan) reuses the best of it. Volume is a ceiling, not a target.

## Fuel
1. **LL Aadi Insights** (n8n table OyCGlRR2PYDdF3DI). Every post must cite at least one insight key. Each insight carries two registers: `insight`/`why` (article voice) and `hook`/`so_what` (social voice, no jargon, ≤14 words).
2. New insights mined weekly from Plaud (the previous 2–3 weeks of client calls) and the Loom videos Aadi sends. Mined rows enter as `active=false`, status "Aadi to confirm", and are listed in the Friday pack.
3. The week's article draft (2–3 posts), the Sunday Snapshot tiles (3 posts), RBA Watch (reactive card).
4. Weekly candid photos from Aadi (Fridays) as card backgrounds once available; shoot footage (first shoot Mon 3 Nov) takes the hero slot, cards fill the gaps.

## Pillars (priority order, from the 31 Oct marketing meetings)
1. **Complex lending** — 3–5 properties, granny flats, equity, structure. Highest value, most differentiated.

## Formats
| Card | Use | Copy limits |
|---|---|---|
| our take | one Aadi view, photo, quote + "what to do with this" | hook ≤14 words, so_what ≤22 |

| myth vs what we see | a common belief vs Aadi's practice | each ≤12 words, because ≤30 |
| question | a question to ask yourself | ≤20 words, body ≤20 |
| carousel | cover + 3–5 points + CTA | point title ≤5 words, body ≤35 |
| rba update | reactive, within hours of a decision | from RBA Watch data only |
| plain english (`term`) | one piece of jargon, explained | term ≤4 words, meaning ≤14, why it matters ≤45 |
| ask the broker (`qa`) | a real question people ask, answered plainly | question ≤18 words, answer ≤55 |
| in his words (`note`) | screenshot-of-a-post text card; threads of 5 for carousels | ≤75 words, 2–4 paragraphs, observation → observation → reframe |

Platforms: LinkedIn = card + 80–150 word text, first line is the hook, no hashtags beyond 3; Instagram = carousel or single card, caption ≤120 words ending with a question, 5 hashtags; Facebook = LinkedIn copy, warmer; WordPress = articles only (existing engine).

## Voice rules (inherited from the article engine, tightened for social)
- Say it the way Aadi says it on a call. Short sentences. "We" voice. No "unlock", "navigate", "journey" as a noun, "leverage" as a verb, "in today's market".
- One idea per post. If it needs a second paragraph, it's two posts or an article.
- Banned: "speak with a broker", "guaranteed", any rate without a date and source, promises about approval, comparisons that name a lender negatively.
- Tax-adjacent lines: "with your accountant". Insurance lines: framed as a question to ask yourself.
- Every card carries "General information only, not personal advice." The CTA slide carries the credit rep line.
- No client details, ever. Anonymise scenarios to the point a client wouldn't recognise themselves.

## Gates (same shape as Draft Article v2)
Write (Opus) → Edit against this doc and platform limits (Sonnet) → independent genericness score 1–5 (Sonnet). Below 4: one rewrite, then held back with a one-line reason. Nothing is scheduled by automation: Zernio drafts only, Aadi schedules.

## Cadence
Ceiling 4 posts per week per platform (3 single cards + 1 carousel) on LinkedIn and Instagram; Facebook is not drafted by the routine. Fewer if the gate holds posts back. An RBA card may go out on decision days in addition.

## Friday pack (one review, Fri 3–4pm Melbourne)
Delivered by the Friday cloud routine: Zernio drafts for the week; new insights to confirm; the Sunday Snapshot draft in HubSpot; the week's article draft in WordPress; what was held back and why. Monthly (first Friday): strategy review from Zernio analytics (saves, shares, profile visits by pillar and card type), what the unmined calls say clients are asking about, and 3 recommendations.
